✧ GOOD TO KNOW

A few little questions.

Everything you need to know before you start making art.

Can I use Pixel Power for free?

Yes. The iOS app offers basic editing, PNG export, and three free looks. More styles and advanced features are available with Pixel Power Pro.

Do I need to make an account?

No account is required to start editing. Choose a photo and create right away.

Does the app upload my pictures?

The version of Pixel Power reviewed for this page processes imported photos on your device and does not send them to a developer-operated server. Your own exports and sharing destinations may have separate policies.

Can I make transparent sprites?

Yes. Pro provides background-removal and sprite-focused features for creating transparent PNG artwork, alongside the core photo-to-pixel editor.

When can I download the app?

Pixel Power is being prepared for an App Store release on iPhone and iPad. The App Store button will become a direct link once the listing is published.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026
Developer: Kelpie Klaws
Contact: kelpieklaws@gmail.com

1. Photos and image editing

You select images with Apple's Photos picker. Imported images and background removal are processed on your device. The reviewed app does not upload imported or edited photos to a developer-operated server, and no user account is required.

2. Saving and sharing

When you save to Photos, the app requests permission to add the PNG. When you share or export to another destination, that destination may handle the exported image under its own privacy practices. Temporary export files are intended to be removed after use or on a later launch.

3. Purchases

Apple processes purchases and subscriptions using StoreKit. The app uses verified Apple entitlement information to unlock paid tools; it does not receive your full payment-card number. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account.

4. On-device information

Editing previews, settings, and some app preferences are stored locally. This version does not offer a developer-operated cloud account or cloud project sync. Deleting the app generally clears its local data, but images already exported remain wherever you saved them.

5. Analytics and advertising

The reviewed app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK, and does not use an advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. Apple and any external apps you use may have their own collection practices.

6. Your choices and contact

You choose which photos to edit or share. If you contact support, your message and email address may be used to respond and retained as appropriate. Contact the support email for privacy inquiries or correspondence deletion requests, subject to legal obligations.

7. Security

The app relies on iOS sandboxing and permissions. No security system can guarantee absolute protection.

8. Updates

This policy may change as the product evolves. The effective date and published policy will be updated accordingly.
