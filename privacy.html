PIXEL MAGICK

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Pixel Magick handles information when you create pixel art on an Apple device.

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Developer: Kelpie Klaws LLC
Privacy contact: kelpieklaws@gmail.com

1. Photos you select

You can choose pictures using Apple’s system Photos picker. The App accesses the images you explicitly select to generate and display pixel art.

Image processing takes place on your device. This includes optional foreground and background separation using Apple’s on-device Vision framework. The current App does not upload selected photos or generated pixel art to a server operated by us.

Your source photo, editing settings, and working previews are held locally while you edit. You do not need an account to use the editing features.

2. Saving and sharing images

When you choose Save to Photos, the App asks for permission to add the generated PNG to your Photos library. It does not request access to your entire library just to save an image. You can change Photos permissions in iOS Settings.

When you use Share PNG or export to another destination, iOS sends the image where you choose. Apple, cloud-sync services, and other apps may process or store it under their own privacy policies and your device settings. We do not control images after you intentionally share them with another service.

For saving and sharing, the App creates a temporary PNG file on your device. It is designed to remove that file when the operation ends and clear leftover export files at the next launch. Images saved to Photos or elsewhere remain there until you or the receiving service delete them.

3. Purchases

Pixel Magick Pro is available as either a one-time Lifetime purchase or a monthly auto-renewing subscription. Apple processes these purchases through StoreKit and the App Store. Pixel Magick checks verified Apple entitlements to grant Pro access. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number.

The monthly subscription renews automatically until canceled in Apple subscription settings. Canceling stops future renewals; access continues for the period Apple recognizes as active. Lifetime is a non-consumable, non-renewing purchase. Purchasing Lifetime does not automatically cancel an existing monthly subscription.

Apple may keep purchase and subscription records and provide developer sales reports, as explained in Apple’s privacy notices. You can restore eligible Pro purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

4. Information on your device

Editing settings and previews stay on your device while you use the App. The current version does not require an account or onboarding profile.

The current App does not offer cloud project syncing or a Pixel Magick account. Deleting the App generally removes its local app data, subject to iOS behavior and backups you control. You must delete images saved to Photos or other destinations separately.

5. Analytics, ads, and tracking

This version of Pixel Magick has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. It does not display third-party ads or use an advertising identifier to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

We do not sell your personal information. Apple’s operating system, the App Store, and services you choose may collect data under their own privacy policies.

6. Retention, deletion, and choices

We do not operate a server that stores your imported photos or generated artwork. You decide which images to keep on your device and where to export them.

You can delete Pixel Magick to remove locally stored preferences and temporary data. You can also decline permission to add images to Photos and continue using the Share PNG option.

For App Store purchase information held by Apple, consult Apple’s privacy and account-management resources.

If you email us, we receive the details you provide, such as your email address and message. We use them to respond to your request. Our email provider may process this correspondence, which we may retain for support or legal obligations. You may request deletion, subject to applicable retention requirements.

7. Security

Pixel Magick relies on iOS app sandboxing and system permissions. Image editing is designed to run on your device.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device, and choose carefully when sharing images with other apps or services.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our features, services, or legal obligations change. We will revise the effective date and make the updated policy available in the App and on our published privacy-policy webpage.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to kelpieklaws@gmail.com.
