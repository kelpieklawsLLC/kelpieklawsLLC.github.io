Pixel Power Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026
Developer: Kelpie Klaws
Privacy contact: kelpieklaws@gmail.com

1. Photos and images you select

You can choose pictures using Apple's system Photos picker. The App accesses the images you explicitly select so that it can generate and display pixel art. Image transformation, including optional foreground/background separation using Apple's on-device Vision framework, takes place on your device. The current App does not upload your selected photos or generated pixel art to a server operated by us.

Your source photo, editing settings, and working previews are held locally while you edit. The App does not require you to create an account to use its editing features.

2. Photos saving, exporting, and sharing

When you choose Save to Photos, the App requests permission to add the generated PNG to your Photos library. It does not request access to your entire photo library merely to save a result. You may change Photos permissions in iOS Settings.

When you choose Share or save an exported file through another destination, iOS sends the image to the destination that you choose. Apple, cloud-sync services, and other apps may process or store shared images according to their own privacy practices and your device settings. We do not control data after you intentionally send it to another service.

To complete a save or share operation, the App creates a temporary PNG file on your device. The App is designed to remove these temporary files after the operation completes and to clear leftover export files when it is next launched. Images you save to Photos or other destinations remain there until you delete them or the receiving service removes them.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

If you purchase Pixel Power Pro, Apple processes the transaction through StoreKit and the App Store. Pixel Power checks Apple's verified purchase-entitlement information to unlock paid features. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number. Apple may maintain purchase records and provide developer sales or subscription reports as described in Apple's own privacy notices.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account's subscription settings. Uninstalling Pixel Power does not, by itself, cancel an Apple subscription.

4. Information stored on your device

Pixel Power uses local app preferences, for example to remember that you have finished the welcome screen. Editing state and previews are held locally during use; the current App does not provide cloud project syncing or a Pixel Power account. Deleting the App generally removes its locally stored app data, subject to iOS behavior and any backups you control. Files you saved to Photos or elsewhere must be deleted from those destinations separately.

5. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The version of the App reviewed for this draft does not include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK, display third-party ads, or use an advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell your personal information. Apple's operating system, App Store, or services you choose to use may collect data under their own privacy policies.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

We do not operate a server that stores your imported photos or generated artwork. You control the images you retain on your device and the destinations to which you export them. To remove locally stored app preferences and temporary data, you may delete Pixel Power. You can also decline Photos add permission and continue using the Share option.

For questions about information held by Apple for App Store purchases, see Apple's privacy and account-management resources. If you contact us at the email address below, we receive the details you send, such as your email address and message, and use them to respond to your request. Support correspondence may be processed by our email provider and kept for as long as reasonably necessary for support or legal obligations. You may ask us to delete your correspondence, subject to any legal retention requirements.

7. Security

Pixel Power relies on iOS application sandboxing and system permissions and is designed to process image edits on the device. No system can provide an absolute guarantee of security. Protect your device and carefully choose the apps and services you share images with.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our features, services, or legal obligations change. We will change the effective date above and make the updated version available in the App and on our published privacy-policy webpage.
