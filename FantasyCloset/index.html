FROM A LITTLE INSPIRATION

Make a portrait
of possibility.

You bring the idea.
Fantasy Closet brings it into the picture.

  1. 01

    Start with you.

    Choose a portrait, or use an existing full-body photo.

  2. 02

    Add what inspires you.

    Bring an outfit reference—or something unexpected, like the cat in our featured demo. Use notes to describe your idea. Fine-tune the hairstyle, shoes, accessories, pose, or expression with optional images and notes.

  3. 03

    See yourself differently.

    Choose a visual style, generate in the app, and save your favorite portraits and comparisons to Photos.
