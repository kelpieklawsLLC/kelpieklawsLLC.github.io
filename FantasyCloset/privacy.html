Privacy notice

Effective October 24, 2026 · Kelpie Klaws LLC

Operator and contact

Fantasy Closet is operated by Kelpie Klaws LLC. For privacy questions or requests about your information, email kelpieklaws@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you provide and use it to respond. Our email provider also processes that correspondence.

What this notice covers

This notice explains how Fantasy Closet handles information when you use the iPhone and iPad app and this website. Updated October 24, 2026.

Photos, references and generation

The app accesses the photos or files you select through the system pickers. When you request a generation or image check, selected images, references, masks, prompts and relevant settings are sent to our backend hosted on Render and then to OpenAI to produce or review your result. Pictures can include faces and visible body features. They are used to create the requested image and check likeness, clothing and visual quality, not to identify you by name. Only upload pictures you have permission to use.

Your device and saved images

The app keeps settings, selected content during use, generated run folders, checkpoints and diagnostic reports on your device. Session and recovery credentials use the iOS Keychain. Saving to Photos requires your permission; sharing sends the files you choose to the destination you select. Your device or cloud backup settings may affect copies of local files.

Anonymous access, credits and purchases

No email sign-in is required for normal use. Our backend creates an anonymous user ID and stores authentication records, credit balances, estimates, usage and billing records, promotion redemptions, and Apple transaction and subscription identifiers. This enables credit recovery, purchase verification, renewals and protection against duplicate charges. Apple handles payment details; the app backend does not receive your full payment card number. Anonymous access is not the same as collecting no data.

Retention

Uploaded source images are processed for requests rather than deliberately saved as a permanent upload library on our backend. Generated response files may be cached for recovery after a connection interruption. The current recovery window is 24 hours, subject to earlier capacity-based eviction. Expired files are removed when cleanup runs, so this is not a guarantee of physical deletion at precisely 24 hours. Job metadata, wallet, transaction and security records may remain longer to operate the service, preserve credits, reconcile purchases and investigate problems. Hosting logs and backups may follow separate retention schedules. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup.

Service providers

Render hosts the backend; OpenAI processes generation and review requests; Apple provides purchases and platform services; GitHub Pages hosts this website. These services process information under their applicable terms and privacy practices, potentially in countries outside yours. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default, unless the API customer opts in; provider safety and retention rules still apply. This is not a promise of zero provider retention.

Diagnostics and website visits

Requests may generate timestamps, request IDs, error details, usage records and network metadata. The backend uses a keyed pseudonym derived from an IP address for some abuse controls; hosting providers may still receive the IP address itself. This static website has no added advertising, analytics scripts, signup forms or tracking cookies. GitHub may process standard request information when serving it. External links take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Your choices and requests

You can remove selected images, manage Photos permissions in iOS Settings, and delete generated run files using the app’s storage controls. This does not delete images already saved to Photos, shared elsewhere, or server-side credit and transaction records. Cancel a subscription through Apple subscriptions; deleting the app does not cancel it. For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, contact the address below. Some financial or security records may need to be retained. Do not send a recovery code, authentication token or payment credentials in a support email.

Security and privacy rights

The app uses HTTPS for its hosted backend connection and iOS protections for local credentials. No system guarantees complete security. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, or complain to a privacy authority. Contact us to make a request; we may need to verify that it relates to your wallet or information, without asking for your secret recovery code.

Changes

We may update this notice as the service changes. The current version and revision date will be available on this page. The app also includes a copy for offline reading.
